Blobby Worlds

The Starkeeper

One night, every star fell from the sky, and the world slipped into gloom. Fierce shadow-beasts — the Gloomblobs — now hoard every last sparkle.

You are the Starkeeper. Gather fallen stars, strike open gloom-cocoons with your Star-Lantern Staff, chain combos and aimed Star Bolts against the Gloomblobs, and carry starlight to the ancient Star Totems to relight the sky.

Wild Starlings are shy: they'll bolt when you come near. Chase one gently until it tires, stay close, and it will join your band. Friends fight beside you and defend themselves — protect them, because Gloomblobs strike back!
